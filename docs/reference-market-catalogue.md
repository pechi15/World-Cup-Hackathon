# Reference Market Catalogue

This catalogue is inspired by publicly visible World Cup prediction-market categories, including the user-provided Polymarket sports page as a taxonomy and UX reference only.

It is not a copy of live prices. Polymarket is not used as a data source, no Polymarket APIs are called, no scraping is performed, and this application is not affiliated with Polymarket.

This catalogue does not assert that TxODDS supports every listed market. Actual supported markets will be replaced or confirmed using authenticated TxODDS responses after credentials are available.

TxODDS TxLINE is the intended future live-data source.

## Match Markets

- Moneyline
- Three-way match result
- Two-way match winner where draws are excluded
- Spread or handicap
- Match totals
- Team totals
- Both teams to score

## Tournament Markets

- Tournament winner
- Reach a specified round
- Stage of elimination
- Group winner
- Group qualification
- Continental winner
- Unbeaten champion

## Player And Team Props

- Golden Boot or top goalscorer
- Player to score
- Most assists
- Player awards
- Team top scorer
- Team tournament performance

## Combination Markets

Combination markets have schema support only in the MVP. Automated pricing and risk aggregation are disabled until explicit correlated settlement rules are approved.
